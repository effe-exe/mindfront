# MindFront audit, 2026-09-14

Question: does everything an AI player is told (`arena/GUIDE.md`, `game_info`, tool descriptions) and shown (`Obs`) match what the engine does, and is anything a player needs missing? Read-only audit of `main` at `f58e55e0b` against `src/core`. Checks run: `npx tsc --noEmit -p .` (clean), `npx vitest run tests/arena` (20/20), `npx tsx arena/decide.ts` (self-check OK).

Severity: **BLOCKER** = a wrong belief or a silent behaviour that changes match outcomes; **WRONG** = stated or shown but not what the engine does; **MISSING** = the engine does it, the player is never told/shown or has no tool; **MINOR** = imprecise, cheap to fix.

Format of every item: what the player is told/shown, what the engine does, evidence, fix.

## 1. Mechanics claims

**M1. BLOCKER. Elimination rule.** GUIDE §1 (l.12): "You are eliminated when an attacker's flood covers your whole territory." Engine: a player holding **fewer than 100 tiles** who loses **one** tile to an attack is conquered outright: all remaining tiles are handed to the attacker (or any non-friendly neighbour) and gold is transferred (`AttackExecution.ts:425-459 handleDeadDefender`, `GameImpl.ts:1322-1402 conquerPlayer`). Spawn gives ~49 tiles (`SpawnExecution`/`Util.ts:145`, radius 4), so every seat starts inside the kill window, and a 90-tile island player dies to the first boat that lands. Fix: GUIDE §1 and §4: "Below 100 tiles, the next tile you lose to an attack ends you and hands over your land and gold; the same is true of every tribe you attack." Add `me.tiles < 100` to `alerts`.

**M2. BLOCKER. Boats do not need a Port.** GUIDE §13 (l.249): "buy a Port early: it opens trade income and every boat you will ever send"; `game_info.units.Port.effect` (`server.ts:72`): "required to launch boats and warships". Engine: a transport ship launches from any shore border tile of the player on the same water component as the landing tile; no Port is involved (`TransportShipUtils.ts:5-31 canBuildTransportShip`, `SpatialQuery.closestShoreByWater` uses `player.borderTiles()`). Only Warships need a Port (`PlayerImpl.ts:1668-1683`). Effect: models spend 125k gold on a Port before their first boat. Fix: both texts: "Port: trade ships + Warships. Boats need only a shore tile."

**M3. BLOCKER. `unclaimedLandAdjacent` is true for every coastal player.** GUIDE §10 (l.196): "whether `expand` is legal at all right now". Computed as `me.sharesBorderWith(game.terraNullius())` (`observe.ts:396`, also the `expand` guard at `observe.ts:500`, the "NO FREE LAND" alert at `observe.ts:397`, and the brain safety net at `brain.ts:719`). Engine: `sharesBorderWith` compares `ownerID`, and water tiles have ownerID 0 = TerraNullius (`PlayerImpl.ts:553-566`, `TerraNulliusImpl.ts:6-8`; the engine's own AI notes this at `AiAttackBehavior.ts:836-838` and uses a land-only check `hasLandBorderWithTerraNullius`). So a coastal player boxed in by rivals is told expand is legal, never gets the alert, and its `expand` calls return `ok:true` while the engine finds no tile and retreats the troops next tick (`AttackExecution.ts:279-283`). Fix: replace with a land-only check (copy `hasLandBorderWithTerraNullius`, early-exit) and derive the alert from it.

**M4. WRONG. Trade needs a rival Port on the same water, and tribes never keep structures.** GUIDE §5 (l.87-91) describes trade as if a Port earns by itself; §13 "opens trade income". Engine: a trade ship only sails to **another player's** Port reachable on the same water component (`PortExecution.ts:99-121 tradingPorts`); tribes delete every structure they own or capture (`TribeExecution.ts:86-94 deleteNextStructure`), nations are disabled in the match config, so the only possible partners are other LLM seats. Allied partners are weighted higher (`PortExecution.ts:138-140`). A Port earns nothing until a rival LLM builds one on the same ocean. Fix: GUIDE §5/§13: "A Port pays only when another AI player has a Port on the same sea; tribes never have Ports." Add `tradePartnerPorts` (count of foreign active Ports sharing a water component with my coast) to `Obs`/`build.Port.note`.

**M5. WRONG. Expansion cost per tile.** GUIDE §12 (l.226-227): "empty tiles cost a flat 5–100 per tile". Engine: attacker loss into terra nullius is `mag/5` = **16 plains / 20 highland / 24 mountain troops per tile** regardless of stack (`Config.ts:820-831`); the 5–100 figure is the speed cost (`within(2000*tileCost/attackTroops, 5, 100)`), which floors once the stack reaches `2000*tileCost/5` = **6,600 (plains) / 8,000 / 10,000 troops**: bigger expansion stacks are not faster, only longer-lived. Fix: state both numbers; add the rule of thumb "expand with 7-10k troops per attack; more only adds duration".

**M6. WRONG. Nuke events never reach any model.** GUIDE §9/§10 promise nukes in `globalEvents`. Brain filters `DisplayEvent` messages with `/nuke|mirv/` (`brain.ts:581`), but detonation messages are the keys `events_display.atom_bomb_detonated` / `hydrogen_bomb_detonated` (`NukeExecution.ts:474-489`) and the inbound warning is a `UnitIncoming` update (`GameImpl.ts:1053-1065`, `TransportShipExecution.ts:153-161`, `NukeExecution.ts:210-225`), which the brain never reads. Nothing about launches, impacts or interceptions is logged or shown. Fix: consume `u[GameUpdateType.UnitIncoming]` (note the recipient seat: "atom bomb inbound from X", "naval invasion incoming from X, N troops") and match `bomb_detonated|missile_intercepted|nukes_destroyed` for `globalEvents`.

**M7. WRONG. Stale unit effects in `game_info`.** `server.ts:75-76` "Missile Silo … there is no launch tool in this arena, so it is inert"; `server.ts:81-82` "Warship … the build tool cannot currently place one". Both tools exist (`server.ts:630-639 nuke`; `observe.ts:622-630` places Warships on water within 3 tiles of a Port, covered by `tests/arena/observe.test.ts:219`). A model that reads `game_info` (the manual tells it to) will never buy a silo or a warship. Fix: rewrite both strings; also state Warship cost ladder and that the Port must be finished (under-construction Ports are not valid launch points, `PlayerImpl.ts:1675-1680`).

**M8. WRONG. Emoji/chat visibility contradicts itself.** GUIDE §9 (l.177-179): "no rival's observation contains them … `recentEvents` and `globalEvents` carry only conquests, alliances, betrayals, nukes, eliminations and the win. Talking is theater, not a channel." GUIDE §10 (l.204) and the code (`brain.ts:587-605`) deliver emoji and quick chats into the recipient's `recentEvents`. Fix: delete the §9 sentence; say "emoji/chat reach the recipient's `recentEvents` only; no mechanical effect".

**M9. WRONG. Rate limit consequence.** `game_info.rateLimits` (`server.ts:457-459`): "Over that you get errors, not actions." Engine: intents over 10/s or 150/min are **silently dropped** by the server (`ClientMsgRateLimiter.ts:4-5`, `SocketIngress.ts:159-175`); the tool still returns `ok:true`. `retreat()` without target sends one intent per running attack (`observe.ts:707-713`). Fix: text "silently dropped"; count intents per seat in `seat.send` and refuse with a reason when the budget is exhausted.

**M10. WRONG. Attack retreats, not "becomes an ally's", when an alliance forms.** GUIDE §4 (l.55-56): "the attack becomes an ally's the moment an alliance forms". Engine: the attack retreats with **no** malus (`AttackExecution.ts:262-266`); a boat in flight simply returns its troops on landing (`TransportShipExecution.ts:262-264`). Fix wording.

**M11. WRONG. `minutesLeft`/`minute` include the spawn phase.** `observe.ts:276` uses `game.ticks()/600`; the engine's timer runs from the end of the spawn phase (`WinCheckExecution.ts:117-126`, `GameImpl.ts:889-906 elapsedGameSeconds`). The clock shown is 20 s ahead. Fix: `max - game.elapsedGameSeconds()/60`.

**M12. WRONG. "bots die much more easily" is x0.7.** `ATTACK_MATH` (`server.ts:93`) and GUIDE §13 (l.252) overstate. Engine: attacker losses x0.7 against a Bot defender (`Config.ts:116,839-844`); the real weakness is their cap (/3) and regen (x0.5) (`Config.ts:960-962,990-992`) and 10k start troops (`Config.ts:928-930`). Fix: give the numbers.

**M13. MISSING. Tribe behaviour is only half described.** GUIDE §8 (l.168-171). Engine (`TribeExecution.ts:38-124`, `AiAttackBehavior.ts:764-810,931-957,1048-1113`): a tribe acts every 40-80 ticks; accepts every alliance request and every extension request; **retaliates first** against its largest non-allied attacker (forced, no troop threshold); hunts a bordering traitor with 1/3 odds, and **breaks its own alliance with an allied traitor** with 1/6 odds; only otherwise attacks once its troops reach 50-60% of its cap, sending everything above a 30-40% reserve; skips LLM neighbours with 50% odds per pick; expands into free land while any borders it; earns 50 gold/tick (`Config.ts:1019-1021`, so a 20-minute-old tribe banks ~600k, all of it loot). Fix: add these five sentences to §8; they decide when a tribe attacks and how much.

**M14. MISSING. Breaking with a traitor is free.** GUIDE §8: every `break_alliance` marks you traitor. Engine: no traitor mark when the other side is currently a traitor (or disconnected) (`GameImpl.ts:836-838`). Fix: one sentence in §8; show `traitorTicksLeft` (`PlayerImpl.ts:853-860 getTraitorRemainingTicks`) for neighbours instead of a boolean.

**M15. MISSING. Immunity is one-directional and you may attack tribes during it.** GUIDE §2 (l.29-32). Engine: only Human attackers respect immunity and only Humans are immune (`PlayerImpl.ts:1880-1899`), so during the 50 ticks you can hit tribes, tribes can hit you, and AI-vs-AI is blocked. Also `nukeSpawn` refuses during immunity (`PlayerImpl.ts:1602-1604`). Fix: state it; the drop reason "immune or otherwise blocked" (`observe.ts:531`) should say how many ticks remain.

**M16. MISSING. Retreat has a 20-tick delay and boats have their own retreat.** GUIDE §4 (l.54-56). Engine: `cancel_attack` freezes the attack immediately and refunds 20 ticks later (`RetreatExecution.ts:3,20-30`, `AttackExecution.ts:243-255`); a boat can be recalled with `cancel_boat` (25% malus on return, `TransportShipExecution.ts:239-259`, `BoatRetreatExecution.ts`), for which there is no tool (T4). Fix: wording + tool.

**M17. MINOR. Surrounded-cluster capture never applies to clusters touching water or the map edge** (`PlayerExecution.ts:182-184,231-233`). GUIDE §4 (l.74-76) omits it. Fix: half a sentence.

**M18. MINOR. Relation direction.** GUIDE §8 (l.150-153) and `Obs.relation` do not say whose opinion it is. `observe.ts:241` reports `me.relation(p)` = **my** stored attitude to them, moved only by what they did to me (`AttackExecution.ts:186`, `BreakAllianceExecution.ts:38-48`); tribes never read relations (`AiAttackBehavior.ts:764-797`). Fix: document as "how you regard them"; for nations it would matter, here it is a diary.

**M19. MINOR. `targets` is a dead field.** `ObsNeighbor.targets` (`observe.ts:251`) lists `targetPlayer` marks that last 100 ticks (`Config.ts:725-727`); no tool sets them and only nations read them (`AiAttackBehavior.ts:373-383`, nations disabled). **M20. MINOR. Overtime mention.** `game_info.winRule` (`server.ts:447-449`) mentions overtime; the match config never enables it (`brain.ts:190-209`, `Config.ts:241-245`). Fix: drop the clause or print `overtime: false`.

**M21. MINOR. "Rate limits are 10 calls a second"** (GUIDE §11 l.222) are intent limits, not tool-call limits; `observe`/`inspect_player` are free. Fix wording.

**M22. MINOR. Structure counts include levels and under-construction units.** `structuresOf` uses `unitCount` = sum of levels including units still under construction (`PlayerImpl.ts:510-529`), while `maxTroops` ignores unfinished Cities (`Config.ts:953-957`). **M23. MINOR. Port/Factory cost note.** GUIDE §13 (l.277): "Do not buy a second Port … the second costs double" is right, but the shared counter with Factories (§6) means a Factory also doubles the next Port. Fix: say "Port and Factory share one price ladder".

## 2. Observation correctness

**O1. WRONG. `freeLandAtBorder` samples the oldest 600 border tiles** (`observe.ts:308-318`). Border-set insertion order keeps the tiles that have been borders longest (coast, static fronts) first, so a large empire whose free land is on its moving frontier reads ~0 exactly when it is big. Same class of cap for `sharedBorderTiles` (first 2000 border tiles, `observe.ts:213-225`): a neighbour beyond the scanned prefix shows `sharedBorderTiles: 0` while sitting in `neighbors`. Fix: stride-sample across the whole set like the build sampler does (`observe.ts:636-637`), or scan fully with an early exit at a count threshold; scans are cheap (`neighbors4` on a typed array).

**O2. WRONG. `coastal` and `reachableByBoat` count lake shores.** `hasCoast` uses `game.isShore` (`observe.ts:61-68`), which is any land next to any water including lakes (`GameMap.ts:367-369`); `isOceanShore` exists (`GameMap.ts:223-234`). A player on a lake appears coastal and boat-reachable; the `boat` translation then fails with "no sea route" after picking that shore. Water components are what the engine checks (`SpatialQuery.closestReachableShore`). Fix: `isOceanShore`, and for `reachableByBoat` require a shared water component (`game.getWaterComponent` on one of my shore tiles vs theirs; cheap with the 300-tile cap).

**O3. WRONG. Boat landing tile is arbitrary.** `translate("boat")` takes the first shore tile in `t.borderTiles()` order (`observe.ts:552-560`), then asks the engine for a water path to it. That tile can be the far side of the target (hundreds of extra tiles of sailing, more warship exposure) or a lake shore (O2). The engine's own AI uses the closest shore pair (`AiAttackBehavior.ts:1116-1123 closestTwoTiles`). Fix: pick the target shore tile nearest my nearest shore tile (or nearest `me.center`), retrying the next candidate when `bestTransportShipSpawn` fails.

**O4. WRONG. `outgoingAttacks[].troops === troopsRemaining`** (`observe.ts:436-444`): both are `a.troops()`. `troops` reads as the launch size and is not. Fix: drop `troops`, add `frontWidth: a.borderSize()`, `retreating: a.retreating()`, `boat: a.sourceTile() !== null` (`Game.ts:386-405`). Same fields for `incomingAttacks`.

**O5. MISSING. Inbound boats and nukes.** A human sees the transport icon and "Naval invasion incoming" / "atom bomb inbound" (`UnitIncoming`, see M6). Cheap: iterate `game.units(UnitType.TransportShip)` whose `targetTile()` I own → `{from, troops, tilesAway}`; nukes in flight from `game.units([AtomBomb, HydrogenBomb, MIRV])` with `targetTile()` owner. Fix: `me.incomingBoats`, `me.incomingNukes`, plus the `note()` on first sight (as done for attacks at `brain.ts:609-617`).

**O6. MISSING. Income by source.** `goldIncomePerMin` (`observe.ts:106-117`) mixes base, trade, trains and one-off loot, so a 500k conquest shows as +500k/min for a minute. `p.tradeGold()`, `p.trainGold()` are lifetime totals (`PlayerImpl.ts:1293-1299`). Fix: `me.income: {base: 6000*mult, tradePerMin, trainsPerMin}` from history deltas of those two, and `me.tradeShipsInFlight`, `tradePartnerPorts` (M4).

**O7. MISSING. Structure levels, ids, construction state.** `structures` is a count per type. A human sees each building's level, whether it is still building, and can upgrade it. Needed for T1. Fix: `me.units: [{id, type, level, underConstruction, x, y}]` (cap 30).

**O8. MISSING. Terrain and Defense Post cover on a front.** Attack cost is x1/x1.25/x1.5 by terrain and x5 under a post (`Config.ts:151-167,810-813`), but the model gets neither per neighbour. Cheap from the `sharedBorderTiles` scan already running: majority terrain of the neighbour's tiles adjacent to my border, and `game.hasUnitNearby(tile, 30, DefensePost, theirId)` on a few sampled front tiles. Fix: `neighbors[].front: {terrain, defensePostCovered, width}`.

**O9. MISSING. Embargoes, outgoing requests, request cooldowns.** `getEmbargoes()`, `outgoingAllianceRequests()`, `canSendAllianceRequest(p)` exist (`PlayerImpl.ts:733-740,795-840,1207-1209`). A model does not know its request is still pending, was rejected/expired (no event either: `brain.ts` ignores `AllianceRequestReply.accepted === false` and `AllianceExpired`), or that a partner cannot be asked for another 300 ticks. Fix: `me.outgoingAllianceRequests: [{id, ticksLeft}]`, `neighbors[].canAlly`, and two `note()`s.

**O10. MISSING. Traitor countdown and alliance partner's other wars.** `isTraitor` is boolean; `getTraitorRemainingTicks()` exists. Fix: `traitorTicksLeft` for me and every viewed player (M14).

**O11. MISSING. Spawn view has no terrain or free-land density.** GUIDE §13 asks to "pick a spawn with a land exit" but `spawnView` (`server.ts:266-288`) shows only majority owner per cell. Fix: per cell `freeLandPct` and `coast: bool` from the same sampling pass; the map is static so compute once.

**O12. MISSING. `map_overview` has no terrain layer.** Mountains and highlands cost 25-50% more and are invisible. Fix: optional `layer: "terrain"` rendering P/H/M per cell (same sampler).

**O13. MINOR. `immuneUntilTick`** is correct (`observe.ts:371-373` matches `GameImpl.ts:882-906`). `goldIncomePerMin` extrapolates from a 50-tick span right after the first sample (`observe.ts:112`), fine but noisy; document.

**O14. MINOR. `build[unit].placeable` for land structures is just "owns land"** (`observe.ts:341-346`); the 15-tile spacing (`Config.ts:1119-1121`) is not checked, so a 60-tile territory with one City lists City as placeable and the tool then drops with "no free spot". Acceptable; make the drop reason say "your territory is too small for another structure: every structure needs a 15-tile radius; upgrade instead" once T1 exists.

**O15. MINOR. `canBuild` lists Warship while the only Port is under construction** (`ports = unitCount(Port)` counts it, `observe.ts:332`), then `toIntents` drops it (`PlayerImpl.ts:1675-1680`). Fix: count `units(Port).filter(u => !u.isUnderConstruction())`.

**O16. MINOR. Cost: fine.** `observe()` is O(border) with caps; `act()` re-observes per call (`server.ts:307`), ~8 observes per round, milliseconds each. `map_overview` samples ≤20k tiles. No hot spots found.

## 3. Tool surface gaps

Intents in `src/core/Schemas.ts:603-723` with no tool, and whether a player needs them:

**T1. MISSING (high). `upgrade_structure`.** Instant, same price as the next unit of that type, no spacing rule (`UpgradeStructureExecution.ts:25-35`, `PlayerImpl.ts:1457-1476`). City +250k cap per level (`Config.ts:337-339,953-958`), Port level = extra trade rolls and dock slots (`PortExecution.ts:73-84`), Silo/SAM level = missiles. In a small or crowded territory it is the only way to raise the cap. GUIDE §6 (l.118) even explains upgrades and then offers no way to do them. Needs O7 for ids. Tool: `upgrade(unit, id?)` (default: the structure of that type nearest my centre).

**T2. MISSING (high). `allianceExtension`.** Both sides calling it resets expiry to now + 5 min, no time window enforced (`AllianceImpl.ts:43-86`, `AllianceExtensionExecution.ts`), and tribes agree automatically (`TribeExecution.ts:73-83`). This makes "quiet border with a tribe for the whole match" a legal, cheap plan. GUIDE §8 (l.161) says "no tool here". Tool: `extend_alliance(target)`; show `allianceExpiry[].theyAgreedToExtend` (`allianceInfo`, `PlayerImpl.ts:762-793`).

**T3. MISSING (medium). `donate_troops` / `donate_gold`.** Allowed to allies only, 100-tick cooldown, config enables it (`PlayerImpl.ts:1066-1121`, `brain.ts:201-202`). Real use: prop up an ally being eaten by the rival you both fear; relation bonus. Tool: `donate(target, troops|gold)`.

**T4. MISSING (medium). `cancel_boat`.** Recall a boat sailing into a warship or a defended shore; 25% malus on return (`BoatRetreatExecution.ts`, `TransportShipExecution.ts:239-259`). Tool: `recall_boats(target?)`.

**T5. MISSING (medium). Boats to unclaimed land and to coastal neighbours.** The engine takes any landing tile owner (`TransportShipExecution.ts:64,84-102`); the arena only accepts `reachableByBoat` ids (the 6 nearest coastal **non-neighbours**, `observe.ts:295-304`, `decide.ts:234-238`). Empty islands and a landing behind a neighbour's lines are impossible, and the 7th-nearest rich tribe is unreachable. Fix: allow any coastal visible id plus `boat_to_land(direction?)` for the nearest free island shore; raise the slice to ~10.

**T6. MISSING (low). `move_warship`** (patrol position = which trade lane it taxes, `WarshipExecution.ts:299-303 patrolRange`). Tool later, with `at`.

**T7. MISSING (low). `embargo` / `embargo_all`**, `delete_unit`, `quick_chat.target` (the "about" player, `QuickChatExecution.ts:13`), `build_unit.amount`. Not needed now. `targetPlayer`: not needed (nations off), see M19.

**T8. WRONG. Ratio cap 0.6.** `RATIO_MAX` (`types.ts:204`) is an arena rule; the engine lets a human send up to 100% and defaults to 20% (`Config.ts:919-925`). Finishing a <100-tile player (M1) or the counter-attack that cancels an incoming stack 1:1 (`AttackExecution.ts:141-154`) can legitimately want more. Fix: raise to 0.9 or expose `troops` as an absolute count; keep the reserve advice in the manual.

**T9. MINOR. Descriptions that under-explain preconditions** (`server.ts:498-639`): `ally` says "to a neighbor" but accepts any visible id, omits 200-tick expiry, 300-tick cooldown and auto-accept when they already asked; `boat` omits that no Port is needed (M2), that the landing tile is chosen for you (O3) and the warship risk; `build` omits 15-tile spacing, build times (20/50/100/300 ticks) and that gold is charged one tick later (G3); `attack` omits that troops leave your pool immediately, the merge/cancel rules and M1; `retreat` omits the 20-tick delay; `nuke` omits own-unit deletion and third-party alliance breaks (G5); `expand` cites the broken flag (M3). Fix: one clause each; the manual is the place for detail, the description must at least name the failure modes.

**T10. MINOR. Drop reasons that do not say what to do instead:** "cannot attack target X right now (immune or otherwise blocked)" (`observe.ts:531`) → say "spawn immunity, N ticks left"; "no free spot for X (keep distance…)" (`observe.ts:695`) → "territory too small for another structure (15-tile radius); grow or upgrade"; "target X has no reachable shore" / "no sea route" (`observe.ts:562-567`) → "try another id in reachableByBoat"; "cannot send an alliance request to X right now" (`observe.ts:591`) → name the cause (cooldown ticks left / your request already pending / they are dead); "already have 3 boats in flight" → give the first ETA.

## 4. Guardrail correctness

**G1. BLOCKER. The idle safety net acts for the player without telling it.** After 300 ticks without an intent, the brain sends `attack{targetID:null, troops:null}` (`brain.ts:658-665,718-721`), i.e. **20% of the army** (`Config.ts:919-925 attackAmount`) into free land, every 30 s, unlogged to the model. A model deliberately holding troops while under attack, or spending its rounds on `observe`/`inspect_player` (which do not reset `lastActionTick`, `brain.ts:307-309`), has a fifth of its army pulled out repeatedly. For a benchmark this is the arena playing. Fix: make it a documented, visible act: `note(seat, "arena auto-expanded with N troops after 30 s idle")`, count `observe` as activity, or remove it (the model already has `expand`) and keep it only for seats whose LLM calls are failing (`seat.errors`).

**G2. WRONG. Auto-spawn is silent.** `autoSpawn` (`brain.ts:651,671-715`) places a seat that did not pick, logs to console only. Fix: `note(seat, "placed at (x,y) by the arena")`.

**G3. WRONG. Double-spend within one tick.** `act()` validates against live gold (`server.ts:305-322`) but the engine charges at `buildUnit` when `ConstructionExecution.tick` runs next tick (`ConstructionExecution.ts:46-69`, `PlayerImpl.ts:1390`). Two `build` calls in one round both pass; the second silently fails (`console.warn`) after returning `ok:true`. Same for two attacks/boats computing `floor(troops*ratio)` from undeducted troops (the engine clamps, `AttackExecution.ts:117-124`). Fix: per-seat `committed: {gold, troops, tick}` in `SeatHandle.send`, subtracted in `sanitize`/`translate` while `tick` is current.
